Everything you need to track your business

Whether you're running a Pisonet, Pisowifi, or any split-revenue operation, Split gives you clarity without cloud subscriptions.

  • Automatic Revenue Splitting

    Set your custom split percentage (e.g., 60/40). Split automatically deducts expenses and calculates exact owner/partner shares.

  • AI Entry Agent

    Just type or speak naturally: "Sales were 5k, spent 300 on router repair." Split parses the data into structured financial records.

  • Financial Dashboard

    Beautiful, responsive interface for analyzing weekly gross sales, expenses, and net revenue with automatic Recharts charting.

  • Intelligence Center

    Automatically generates monthly summaries of operational notes to spot business trends and track maintenance events over time.

  • Privacy-First (Local)

    Powered by a local SQLite database. No cloud dependencies required. Your financial data stays securely on your own machine.

  • Export & Backup

    Export your ledger to CSV for accountants, and backup your entire database year-by-year as simple JSON files.

Meet Split AI

Don't want to fill out forms? Just tell Split what happened. The integrated AI parses natural language into precise ledger entries.

  • "Made ₱5000 this week, but had to replace a ₱300 router."
  • "Just ₱2000 gross. Spent ₱100 on load."

Plus, the Intelligence Center reads your notes to summarize monthly performance and flag operational issues.
